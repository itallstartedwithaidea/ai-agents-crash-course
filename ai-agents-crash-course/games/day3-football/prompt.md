# 🎮 Day 3: Football (Tecmo Bowl)

> Copy → Paste into [Claude](https://claude.ai) or [Cursor](https://cursor.com) → Play

```
Build an American football arcade game (Tecmo Bowl style) as a single HTML file. Requirements:
- Top-down football field with yard lines and end zones
- Player controls quarterback/ball carrier with arrow keys
- 5 offensive and 5 defensive players per play
- Play selection screen with 4 offensive plays
- Pass mechanic: spacebar to throw, arrow keys to aim
- Tackle detection: defender sprite contact with ball carrier
- Complete scoring, downs system (1st–4th), and yard tracking
- Touchdown celebration animation
- 8-bit pixel art aesthetic
```

**Controls:** Arrows move/aim · Space pass/select

**Iterate:** "Add punt on 4th down" · "Add 2-player mode" · "Add stamina system"
